Share of searches where the top result is exactly what was asked for, on Amazon's public shopping-search test (8,956 searches). ShopRank is a small AI model that re-orders search results so the products people want come first. It runs on a laptop CPU, no GPU needed.

Example: search iphone xs pro. In keyword search, the first right product is number 12 (below the 8 shown). ShopRank puts 3 right products first. See both lists

How it works

  1. Big teacher. Five DeBERTa models learn from Amazon's labelled searches. Accurate, but too slow for a normal computer.

  2. Small student. A small model learns to copy the teachers and keeps most of their skill.

  3. Compressed. Shrunk to 32 MB with almost no loss, it ranks a typical list of 16 products in about 0.07 seconds (median) on a laptop CPU.

  4. Typo fix. Typos hurt the ranking, so a spell checker fixes the search first. On made-up one-letter typos it wins back almost 90% of the accuracy that typos cost.

Where it fails

  • Exact product names can trip it up: for nintendo switch console it put the cheaper Switch Lite first, while keyword search found the console.
  • On long, chat-style requests the big ready-made models can rank better (see the Chromebook example).
  • The spell checker sometimes “fixes” real names: nivda (Nvidia) became nivea.
  • It does not search the whole catalogue: it re-orders the results another search returns, and only for English (US) searches.
For engineers: method and exact numbers
  • Task and metric. Amazon Shopping Queries (ESCI) Task 1, US. Full-list nDCG with gains Exact 1, Substitute 0.1, Complement 0.01, Irrelevant 0. Keyword baseline: BM25 on product titles.
  • Test discipline. Official test (8,956 queries) scored once after pre-registration; 95% query-bootstrap intervals and a trec_eval cross-check in the README. The examples above come from the dev split.
  • Teacher. 5-fold DeBERTa-v3-base cross-encoders, mean score: 0.8877 [0.8849, 0.8905].
  • Student. MiniLM-L12 (33M parameters) distilled with MSE on each pair's out-of-fold teacher score: 0.8674 [0.8643, 0.8703], +0.013 [+0.011, +0.015] over Amazon's MiniLM recipe re-run on the same queries (0.8544).
  • Serving. ONNX Runtime dynamic INT8 (32 MB, dev -0.0008 vs fp32), FastAPI in Docker; p50 69 ms / p95 232 ms per request (median 16 candidates) on a 16-thread laptop CPU.
  • Typos. One synthetic typo costs 0.028 dev nDCG. A one-edit spell corrector over the catalogue vocabulary recovers 87-90% on dev and dev2 with no measurable loss on clean queries; typo-augmented training recovered 14% and was rejected.
  • Ready-made rerankers. Zero-shot BGE-reranker-v2-m3 (568M) 0.8585 and Qwen3-Reranker-0.6B 0.8540 on dev, both below the served model (paired CIs exclude 0).
